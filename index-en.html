02 / Research

Research Experience

3D Gaussian Splatting
June 2026

SAOP-LRSH for CompressionGS

Scale-aware opacity pruning with low-rank spherical harmonics compression for compact 3D Gaussian Splatting.

  • Added scale-aware opacity pruning to remove low-contribution Gaussians more aggressively during late-stage training.
  • Implemented low-rank SH compression to reduce spherical-harmonic storage while preserving rendering quality.
  • Built reproducible experiments and batch evaluation for the chair, ficus, lego, and materials scenes.

7-DOF Robotic Manipulators
June 2025 - June 2026

Redundancy Resolution and Manipulability Optimization for 7-DOF Robotic Manipulators

Investigated configuration selection and dexterity preservation under end-effector pose constraints through kinematic modeling, singularity analysis, and optimization.

  • Established DH-based kinematic models and derived Jacobian matrices.
  • Analyzed singular configurations and their relationship with the Yoshikawa manipulability measure.
  • Studied null-space projection and trajectory-level manipulability optimization.
