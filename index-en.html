02 / Research

Research Experience

June 2025 - Present

7-DOF Robotic Manipulators

Redundancy Resolution and Manipulability Optimization for 7-DOF Robotic Manipulators

This work investigates configuration selection and dexterity preservation for redundant manipulators under end-effector pose constraints through kinematic modeling, singularity analysis, and optimization.

  • Established DH-based kinematic models for redundant 7-DOF manipulators and derived their Jacobian matrices.
  • Analyzed singular configurations using matrix rank and determinant-based methods, and investigated the relationship between robot configurations and the Yoshikawa manipulability measure.
  • Studied null-space projection for redundancy resolution and explored strategies for maintaining high manipulability along motion trajectories.
